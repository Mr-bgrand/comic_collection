## Card method

The 22 TAG values use the owner's authorized exact-card, same-numeric-grade PSA comparison. There was no verified direct TAG value for these grades. PriceCharting's generic Grade 8/9 buckets were not used: individual completed-sale titles were checked for PSA grade, card number, edition, year and language.

Each median uses up to ten of the most recent unambiguous listings observed within the last 90 days (June 20–September 18). Across the cards, **181 distinct PSA transactions** were reviewed; the two owned Wailord copies share the same ten sales but have separate copy-scoped observations. Prices are source-reported USD sale amounts, without invented shipping/tax adjustments. A median ending in half a cent is rounded to cents.

Excluded examples include Mimikyu jumbo cards, a conflicting Rika Japanese-language title, Salamence collection/stamped promos, Noibat titles with another card number, ambiguous multi-card quantities, other grading companies and rows with an original offer price. This is a reviewed sample of the page's visible history, not a claim of exhaustive sales coverage.

Bombirdier has four recent matches, Rika four, and Salamence three, so all carry \*. Cleffa TAG 8.5 remains unresolved; neither PSA 8 nor PSA 9 is substituted.
