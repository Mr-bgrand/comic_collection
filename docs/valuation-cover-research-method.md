# Cover-first valuation research

A failed catalog-name search is not evidence that no comparable exists. The November15 text-only pass missed w0rldtr331 No Masss third printing because sellers use Oshred Virgin Third Print SDCC2023 LE250. Earlier unresolved entries require this deeper review.

1. Inspect the owned front/back and slab label. Record artist credit, artwork composition, homage, printing, finish, signature/label category and any barcode or edition statement. Do not infer raw condition.
2. Build alternate searches from artist names and verified pseudonyms, store names/spelling variants, convention, print run, and visual descriptions. Keep CGC's original variant name; aliases supplement it.
3. Use image search to discover seller/catalog names and visually compare actual listing photographs. Seller titles may omit or misname the variant. Check the slab label where available; distinguish visually similar printings, finishes and signatures.
4. Open the candidate listing. When a text fetch fails, use the browser. Capture current amount, currency, listing ID, cert, availability, sale date if actually sold, and shipping separately. A search index may be stale or show an ongoing bid rather than a buy-now price.
5. Save validated aliases and source links. Grade and label matching still apply after identity is established. A9.8 offer does not price the owned9.6 copy. Mark unresolved entries with the specific remaining investigation, not a claim that all sources are exhausted.

## November 22: owner-authorized comparative appraisals

When exact evidence is absent, a documented low-confidence appraisal may use nearby grades, other graders, raw anchors or related labels/editions. Describe every mismatch, preserve original source facts, and separate judgment adjustments from measured price differences. Use an owner-basis observation for the appraised copy, explicitly accept it, mark it with an asterisk and include it in the total. Target identity matching does not assert that underlying comparables match. Save a working range, confidence, scarcity evidence and explanation. Do not invent print runs, use population as production count, or assume scarce means valuable. Prefer conservative anchors without unsupported premiums. Locked values stay locked; unassessed raw references stay outside the total. See [first appraisals](valuation-2026-11-22-provisional-appraisals.md).
